# 01 HMI Cross Domain Standards Study v0

Source: `90_System/Knowledge/Human_Factors/01_HMI_Cross_Domain_Standards_Study_v0.md`

## Stable takeaways

- HMI is a task-control interface, not a passive information display.
- Situation awareness is the main design goal.
- Alarms must be actionable.
- Critical operations require gating.
- Automation must be visible and overridable.
- Degraded mode is a normal case, not an edge case.
- Consistency matters more than local novelty.
- The design must leave review evidence.
