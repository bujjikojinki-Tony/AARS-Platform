# CDA Glossary Baseline

## Core Terms

### CDA
Working shorthand for the pilot project domain represented across [[03_Projects/CDA/Pilot_001_CDA/CDA_Project_Home|CDA Project Home]].

### Research Question
The bounded question that organizes the pilot scope, working assumptions, and downstream outputs.

### Claim
A candidate assertion that may later be tested, refined, or limited through review and evidence.

### Corpus
The set of materials or observations considered relevant to the pilot analysis.

### Analytic Unit
The basic unit of observation, coding, or interpretation inside the project workflow.

### Analytic Lens
The interpretive perspective used to read patterns, tensions, or structures in the corpus.
