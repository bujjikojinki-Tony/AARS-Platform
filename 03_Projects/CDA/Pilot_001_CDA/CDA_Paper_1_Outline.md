# CDA Paper 1 Outline

## 1. Working Title

**Toward a Structured Conceptual Baseline for Critical Digital Assets Research**

---

## 2. Paper Role in the Roadmap

This is **Paper 1** in the `CDA_3_Paper_Roadmap`.

Its role is to establish the **conceptual baseline** for CDA research.

It should:
- define the CDA problem space
- stabilize key vocabulary
- separate CDA from adjacent but non-identical concepts
- provide a taxonomy-oriented conceptual foundation
- prepare the ground for later layered architecture work

It should **not**:
- become the full architecture paper
- become the applied domain paper
- attempt complete framework synthesis

---

## 3. Core Research Question

**How should Critical Digital Assets be conceptually defined, typed, and distinguished from adjacent concepts so that CDA research can proceed on a stable and reusable basis?**

---

## 4. Main Objective

To establish a bounded, structured, and reusable conceptual baseline for CDA research that is sufficient to support later architecture expression and domain-oriented application work.

---

## 5. Primary Contributions

This paper is expected to make the following contributions:

### Contribution 1
A clarified conceptual definition of **CDA** as a research object rather than a loose operational label.

### Contribution 2
A structured distinction between:
- CDA
- Digital Asset
- Criticality
- Risk
- Mission Impact
- Dependency
- Cybersecurity-related concepts

### Contribution 3
A baseline taxonomy for organizing CDA-related concepts into stable categories.

### Contribution 4
An explicit identification of concept-layer tensions that must be preserved rather than prematurely collapsed.

---

## 6.2 Problem Framing

### Purpose
Explain the conceptual problem space.

### Should Cover
- ambiguity around what counts as a digital asset
- ambiguity around what makes a digital asset “critical”
- confusion between CDA and cybersecurity-only framing
- confusion among concept, property, risk, and consequence

The problem is not lack of importance; the problem is lack of structured conceptual discipline.

## 12. Draft Abstract Skeleton

### Version Skeleton
This paper addresses the conceptual instability surrounding Critical Digital Assets (CDA) research. Rather than assuming CDA as a self-evident or purely cybersecurity-defined category, the paper develops a bounded conceptual baseline for CDA as a structured research object. It clarifies the relationship between CDA and broader digital asset notions, distinguishes evaluative concepts such as criticality from consequence concepts such as risk and mission impact, and proposes a baseline taxonomy for organizing CDA-related concepts. The paper further identifies unresolved boundary cases that should remain explicit in subsequent work. The result is a reusable conceptual foundation intended to support later layered architecture expression and domain-oriented CDA research.
