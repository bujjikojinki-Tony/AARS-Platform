## 3. What This Round Attempted
本轮只尝试处理三个 P3 gaps：

1. Outer-Scope Re-entry Strictness Gap  
2. Historical Similar-Case Evidence Use Gap  
3. Narrative Override Risk Gap  

本轮的策略是：
- 不改变 P1/P2 已建立的主规则结构
- 不增加新结构层
- 只增强边界纪律、supporting evidence 使用纪律和长期维护中的抗漂移纪律
- 优先防止“规则被慢慢稀释”，而不是追求更多升级路径

---

## 4. What Was Achieved

### 4.1 Outer-Scope Re-entry Was Tightened
当前已明确：
out-of-scope 对象若要重入主链，必须同时具备：
- bounded linkage explicit
- non-peripheral relevance shown
- new evidence introduced

这意味着外围对象不再能凭“看起来相关”或旧材料重回主链。

### 4.2 Historical Similar-Case Evidence Was Restricted
当前已明确：
historical similar-case evidence 只允许作为 **bounded supporting evidence supplement** 使用，且必须满足：
- analogy boundary explicit
- non-substitution rule
- support-only positioning

这意味着类比证据现在不能替代当前 case 的 G3/G4/G5/G6 required evidence。

### 4.3 Narrative Override Protection Was Added
当前已明确：
当 narrative statement 强于当前 rule status 时，必须优先回看：
- evidence granularity
- propagation status
- disposition status

并在必要时触发：
- narrative override caution
- wording correction
- update log correction item

这意味着长期维护中“叙事把握压过规则门槛”的风险被正式纳入控制。
